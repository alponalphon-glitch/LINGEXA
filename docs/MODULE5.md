# Module 5 — Real-Life Word Quest

- 100 sequential levels, grouped into 10 themed worlds.
- 10 distinct English vocabulary entries per level (1,000 unique entries in this module's word bank).
- Each round presents the target word, a short example sentence, text-to-speech controls, and a four-choice recognition test.
- Correct answers award XP and gems; each completed level unlocks the next.
- Per-level step and unlocked-level state are stored in SharedPreferences under `m5_step_*` and `module5_unlocked`.
- Module 5 is unlocked after all 100 levels of Module 4 are completed.

The word bank is an English pronunciation-dictionary-derived list; meanings are currently generic study guidance rather than curated Tajik translations. Android build/device verification remains outstanding.
