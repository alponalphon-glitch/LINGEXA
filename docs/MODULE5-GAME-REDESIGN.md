# Module 5 — Real-Life Word Quest (Iteration 19)

- 100 levels with 10 questions per level.
- The blue owl teacher mascot appears on every question and the empty-energy screen.
- Learning interface, prompts, vocabulary, examples, and buttons are in English.
- Teacher feedback and encouragement are in Russian; no Tajik strings were added to Module 5.
- Five shared hearts are displayed. A wrong answer removes one heart; one heart regenerates every 30 minutes using the existing persistent heart timer.
- At zero hearts, the lesson is paused and the learner sees the refill timer.
- Correct answers award 7 XP and 1 Gem. Completing a level awards 20 XP and 4 Gems.
- Per-level question progress is saved; completing all 100 levels unlocks Module 6.

The existing Module 5 word bank and meaning/example generation were preserved. Android build/device testing has not been performed as part of this change.
